# What makes it special

Starlink-style systems combine mass-produced satellites, electronically steered **phased-array** user terminals, dense LEO shells, and (increasingly) **inter-satellite links**. Capacity is still shared — more satellites help coverage and capacity, but they are not infinite bandwidth.
