# Why LEO? Starlink in context

Starlink is a **Low Earth Orbit (LEO)** broadband system: thousands of satellites, user terminals on the ground, and gateways that connect space to the terrestrial internet.

This module will grow into the full on-ramp: GEO vs LEO, system pieces, and why a *constellation* is required.

## The one-sentence idea

GEO can cover a continent with one satellite — but it’s far away. LEO is close (lower latency, smaller terminals) — but each satellite races across the sky, so you need **many** of them.

<Advanced title="Light-time sketch">
  Distance and the speed of light set a hard floor on latency. One-way time is roughly $t = d / c$. A GEO hop is on the order of a tenth of a second one-way; a LEO hop is a few milliseconds one-way to the satellite (routing and processing add more).
