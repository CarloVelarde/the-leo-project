# Orbits without fear

Altitude decides how long a circular orbit takes and how fast a satellite moves. You do not need prior orbital mechanics — only curiosity and a bit of algebra.

<Advanced title="Kepler period (circular orbit)">
  For a circular orbit with semi-major axis $a$ (Earth radius + altitude) and Earth gravitational parameter $\mu$:

  $$
  T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{a^3}{\mu}}
  $$

  The lab insight panel uses this formula.
</Advanced>
